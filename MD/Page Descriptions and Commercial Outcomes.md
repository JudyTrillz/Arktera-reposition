# Page Descriptions & Commercial Outcomes

Every description below is 1–2 sentences and matches what's actually live in each page's `<meta name="description">` tag (see `src/lib/seo.ts`, `src/lib/insights.ts`, and each `page.tsx`) — this file is a readable map of that same content, not a separate draft.

For each page: what it's about, what search intent it's built to catch, and the commercial outcome that intent should lead to.

---

## Core Pages

### Home — `/`

**Description (meta tag):** Arktera Systems helps service businesses uncover and fix the problems stopping them from getting more customers, winning more sales, and growing revenue.

**What's on the page:** Hero framing the core offer, a "the problem you're fixing may not be the problem you have" section, the six-stage Revenue System explained, Arktera's five-step process (Assess → Diagnose → Design → Build → Measure), who Arktera works with, an Insights teaser, and a final CTA.

**Search intent:** Branded lookups ("Arktera Systems") and broad commercial-investigation queries from a business owner who senses something's wrong but can't name it ("why has my revenue stalled," "sales not converting," "revenue systems consultant").

**Commercial outcome:** Visitor starts the **Free Business Growth Assessment** — the homepage's job is to earn that first click for someone who hasn't yet defined their problem.

---

### About — `/about`

**Description (meta tag):** Arktera Systems diagnoses the constraints holding back revenue and designs practical solutions that strengthen commercial performance for service businesses.

**What's on the page:** Why "doing more" isn't the same as fixing the right thing, Arktera's philosophy ("the business comes first, the solution follows"), how the six revenue stages interact, the five-stage process in more depth, who Arktera is built to help, and a founder's-perspective note from Judy Odiakose.

**Search intent:** Credibility research from someone already evaluating Arktera against alternatives ("who is Arktera Systems," "is this consultancy legit," "Arktera Systems approach").

**Commercial outcome:** Visitor's hesitation is resolved and they proceed to the **Free Business Growth Assessment** — About exists to convert trust into action, not to be a dead end.

---

### The Revenue System — `/revenue-systems`

**Description (meta tag):** See how demand, trust, conversion, delivery, retention, and referral work together, and identify what's holding your revenue system back.

**What's on the page:** Why fixing one part of the business in isolation doesn't work, the six stages explained in depth (what each should achieve, where it breaks, the commercial impact), how the framework applies by industry, and how it turns into actual implementation work.

**Search intent:** Commercial-investigation queries from someone researching the *method*, not yet the vendor ("revenue system framework for service business," "why do leads not convert to sales," "demand trust conversion delivery retention referral").

**Commercial outcome:** Visitor applies the framework to their own business by starting the **Free Business Growth Assessment** — this page sells the method so the next click sells itself.

---

### Free Business Growth Assessment — `/business-growth-assessment`

**Description (meta tag):** Know what's holding your business back. Complete Arktera's Free Business Growth Assessment to share your goals and challenges for a focused conversation.

**What's on the page:** What the assessment covers and what happens after submitting, then the four-stage form itself (business profile, objectives, challenges, performance data).

**Search intent:** High-intent, ready-to-act searches from someone who wants help now ("free business growth assessment," "diagnose why my business isn't growing," "revenue audit for service business").

**Commercial outcome:** **Completed, submitted assessment** — this is the site's primary lead-generation conversion, the one every other page is built to funnel toward.

---

### Insights — `/insights`

**Description (meta tag):** Practical analysis on revenue performance, conversion, profitability, and operational efficiency, helping service businesses find out what to fix next.

**What's on the page:** Latest articles, featured articles, and a filterable archive by category (Fixing Revenue Leaks, Protecting Profit Margins, Improving Pipeline Conversion, Strengthening Client Retention), ending in a CTA.

**Search intent:** Top-of-funnel, symptom-specific informational searches from someone who hasn't connected their symptom to a named problem yet ("why is my revenue not growing," "sales process improvement tips").

**Commercial outcome:** Visitor opens an article that matches their specific symptom, then that article's own CTA carries them to the **Free Business Growth Assessment** — Insights exists to capture search traffic the core pages can't.

### Contact — `/contact`

**Description (meta tag):** Have a question about Arktera, our approach, or how we work? Send us a message and we'll respond within one business day.

**What's on the page:** A direct contact form, "what happens next" (response within one business day), the direct email address, and a cross-sell into the Free Business Growth Assessment for anyone who actually wants a business review rather than just an answer.

**Search intent:** Lower-commitment, question-first intent — not yet ready for a full assessment, or wants a human answer first ("contact Arktera Systems," "ask a question before I commit").

**Commercial outcome:** **Message submitted** as the immediate goal, with the page itself nudging higher-intent visitors toward the **Free Business Growth Assessment** as the better-fit next step.

---

### Terms of Service — `/terms-of-service`

**Description (meta tag):** The terms governing use of the Arktera Systems website and engagement with our services.

**Search intent:** Compliance/verification lookups, almost never organic discovery — typically a lead double-checking terms before submitting a form, or a crawler/bot.

**Commercial outcome:** None directly. Its job is to remove a trust objection quietly in the background so the Contact and Assessment forms convert without friction.

---

### Privacy Policy — `/privacy-policy`

**Description (meta tag):** How Arktera Systems collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information.

**Search intent:** Same as Terms of Service — a trust/compliance check, not a buying search.

**Commercial outcome:** None directly; supports conversion elsewhere by answering "what happens to my data" before someone submits the Assessment form (which asks for real business data).

---

### Page Not Found — `/404`

**Description (meta tag):** The page you're looking for may have moved, been renamed, or no longer exists. *(Marked `noindex` — this page is never meant to be a search landing page.)*

**What's on the page:** A clear "this route doesn't exist" message plus two recovery paths — start the Free Business Growth Assessment, or return to the homepage — and secondary links into Revenue Systems and Insights.

**Search intent:** None — arrival is always via a broken or outdated link, not a search.

**Commercial outcome:** Recapture the visitor rather than lose them: redirect toward the **Free Business Growth Assessment** or deeper into the site instead of letting a dead link become a bounce.

---

## Summary: One Funnel, One Conversion

Every page on the site — core pages, all 12 articles, even the 404 — ends up pointing at the same place: the **Free Business Growth Assessment**. Contact is the only page with a genuinely separate outcome (a direct message), and even it cross-sells the Assessment to anyone whose question is really "can you fix my business." Terms and Privacy carry no commercial outcome of their own; they exist to protect the conversions happening everywhere else.
