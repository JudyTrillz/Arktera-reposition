3. Revenue systems page:
| Order | Section                                          | Purpose                                                                                                               | Key execution decision                                                                          |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | **Revenue Systems Hero**                         | Define the Arktera Revenue System and its relevance to business performance.                                          | Lead with the business outcome and the connected-system concept. Avoid guarantees.              |
| 2     | **The Systems Reality**                          | Explain why weaknesses in one business function can undermine results elsewhere.                                      | Use clear business examples rather than unsupported mathematical claims.                        |
| 3     | **The Six Stages of the Arktera Revenue System** | Present Demand, Trust, Conversion, Delivery, Retention, and Referral as connected stages.                             | Use a clear system diagram, not six disconnected service cards.                                 |
| 4     | **Explore the Six Stages**                       | Help visitors understand each stage's objective, common problems, commercial implications, and possible improvements. | Use compact, consistent blocks with expandable details or other scan-friendly layouts.          |
| 5     | **Industry Alignment**                           | Show how the framework can be applied to different business models.                                                   | Focus on industry-specific considerations, not repeated industry introductions.                 |
| 6     | **How the Framework Becomes Action**             | Connect the six revenue stages to Assess → Diagnose → Design → Build → Measure.                                       | Clearly distinguish what Arktera examines from how it works.                                    |
| 7     | **Final CTA**                                    | Invite appropriate prospects to take the first step.                                                                  | Use the official Free Business Growth Assessment name and an accurate description of the offer. |
