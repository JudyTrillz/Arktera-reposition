**Content requirement**

# Stage 3: Business Challenges

Every business faces different constraints. Tell us which challenges you are currently experiencing so we can better understand what may be affecting your business performance.

### 1. Which challenges are you currently experiencing in your business?

**Field type:** Multiple selection
**Required:** Yes
**Selection limit:** Select up to 5

Choose the challenges that best describe your current situation. You do not need to know what is causing them.

- **Not Enough Qualified Inquiries**
  We are not receiving enough inquiries from people who are a good fit for our services.
- **Inconsistent Inquiry Volume**
  The number of inquiries fluctuates, making it difficult to plan or predict demand.
- **Too Many Unqualified Inquiries**
  We receive inquiries, but many are not a good fit for our services, pricing, or requirements.
- **Inquiries Are Not Turning into Clients**
  People express interest in our services, but too few become paying clients.
- **Slow or Inconsistent Follow-Up**
  We struggle to respond promptly, follow up consistently, or keep potential clients engaged.
- **Sales Opportunities Are Being Lost**
  Potential clients lose interest, delay decisions, choose alternatives, or disappear before committing.
- **Revenue Is Too Low or Unpredictable**
  Revenue is below our expectations or varies too much to plan confidently.
- **Profit Margins Are Under Pressure**
  Costs, pricing, discounts, or the resources required to deliver our services are affecting profitability.
- **Our Prices May Not Reflect the Value or Cost of Delivery**
  We are uncertain whether our pricing adequately accounts for the value we provide and the cost of delivering our services.
- **Existing Clients Rarely Return**
  We struggle to generate repeat business or additional engagements from existing clients.
- **Too Few Referrals**
  We receive fewer referrals than we would like from satisfied clients or business relationships.
- **Client Payments Are Delayed**
  Late payments or outstanding invoices make it difficult to manage cash flow.
- **Service Delivery Is Inefficient or Difficult to Scale**
  Our processes, workload, or available capacity make it difficult to serve clients efficiently or handle more business.
- **We Lack Reliable Business Performance Data**
  We struggle to measure or understand performance across inquiries, sales, revenue, profitability, or client retention.
- **Our Business Processes Are Too Manual or Disconnected**
  We rely on manual work, scattered information, or disconnected tools to manage important business activities.
- **Other**
  We are experiencing a challenge not listed above.
- **I'm Not Sure What the Main Challenge Is**
  I know we need to improve business performance, but I am not yet sure what is holding us back.
