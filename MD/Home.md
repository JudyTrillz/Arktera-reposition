## 4. The Arktera Revenue System

# The Arktera Revenue System

## Revenue Is Won or Lost at Six Stages.

Your business can lose revenue before a customer buys, while you're serving them, or after the sale.

Each stage presents opportunities to win customers, protect profit, and build lasting relationships. A weakness in one area can limit the results of the entire business.

### 01. Demand

**Are the right people finding you?**

If potential customers aren't discovering your business, you may struggle to generate enough enquiries and sales.

_Where revenue gets lost: poor visibility, weak targeting, or marketing that attracts attention but few suitable buyers._

### 02. Trust

**Do they have a reason to choose you?**

People need to understand what you offer, why it matters, and why they should trust your business.

_Where revenue gets lost: unclear offers, weak proof, or unanswered concerns that make potential customers hesitate._

### 03. Conversion

**Are enquiries turning into paying customers?**

Interest alone doesn't generate revenue. Your follow-up, sales process, and booking experience all influence whether an enquiry becomes a sale.

_Where revenue gets lost: missed enquiries, slow responses, inconsistent follow-up, or a difficult path to purchase._

### 04. Delivery

**Can you deliver well and protect your profit?**

Winning customers is only part of the job. Your pricing, costs, capacity, and processes determine how well your business serves them and what it keeps.

_Where revenue gets lost: underpricing, wasted resources, delivery bottlenecks, or costs that eat into margins._

### 05. Retention

**Do customers have a reason to return?**

A good customer relationship shouldn't end at the first sale. The right experience and follow-up can create opportunities for repeat business.

_Where revenue gets lost: poor aftercare, inconsistent communication, or no process for encouraging customers to return._

### 06. Referral

**Are satisfied customers bringing others to you?**

Happy customers can become a valuable source of new business when you make it easy for them to recommend you.

_Where revenue gets lost: missed opportunities to request reviews, ask for introductions, or build relationships with repeat referrers._
